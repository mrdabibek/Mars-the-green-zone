# Downloaded assets and provenance
All files are candidate assets, not yet visually or technically approved for the game. Verify scale, pivots, collisions, materials, optimization, legal use and UE import before integration. No rig or animation has been confirmed.

- NASA Z2 spacesuit: `References/Z2_Spacesuit_NASA.glb` — https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/z2-spacesuit/ — NASA/LaRC/Advanced Concepts Lab. Check NASA media/insignia rules. Static/rig status unverified.
- NASA Habitat Demonstration Unit: `References/Habitat Demonstration Unit (part 1).glb` and `(part 2).glb` — https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/habitat-demonstration-unit/ — NASA/LaRC/Advanced Concepts Lab. Habitat reference; interior suitability unverified. Check NASA media/insignia rules.
- Poly Haven `potted_plant_04` and `potted_plant_02`: 2K FBX and accompanying textures in `PolyHaven/`. https://polyhaven.com/a/potted_plant_04 and https://polyhaven.com/a/potted_plant_02 — CC0: https://polyhaven.com/license . Mature decorative plants, not growth-stage sets.
- Poly Haven `moon_rock_01` and `moon_rock_02`: 2K FBX and accompanying textures in `PolyHaven/`. https://polyhaven.com/a/moon_rock_01 and https://polyhaven.com/a/moon_rock_02 — CC0: https://polyhaven.com/license . Lunar rocks as environment candidates; not claimed Mars geology.

Poly Haven downloads checked against API MD5. Models have not been inspected in Unreal. FBX material and texture links may need manual setup.
